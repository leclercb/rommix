# 07: An installed game keeps the system it was installed under

> _This was generated by AI during triage._

**Status:** needs-triage

**What happened:** a Switch 2 game installed while its platform resolved to `switch` stayed `switch` after RomMix learned `switch-2` → `switch2`: same folder (`roms/switch/`), same emulator, and its saves could not sync. The user had to fix settings and reinstall.

**Established:**

- Launch reads `installed.system` (`src/main/ipc/game.ts`), recorded once at install by `resolveSystem` (`src/main/library.ts`); nothing re-resolves it later.
- In this case the old resolution came from a `systemOverrides` entry and an `emulatorPaths.eden` pointing at Eglantine, both set by the user before Switch 2 support existed.
- The same trap applies to any future change of a slug's mapping.

**Open:** a product decision — notice the mismatch and offer to move the game? Move silently? Only warn? What happens to saves already written under the old system? Needs `/grill-with-docs` rather than a direct brief.
